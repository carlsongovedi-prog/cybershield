# CyberShield — Professional and Ethical Boundaries

## Project Information

- Project ID: AISEC-001
- Organization: CyberShield Security Solutions
- Document Type: Professional Conduct and Ethics
- Status: Active Guideline

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the ethical, legal and professional boundaries that CyberShield personnel must follow when conducting cybersecurity and AI security assessments.

## 2. Authorization

- Obtain explicit written permission before testing any system.
- Confirm the identity and authority of the person granting permission.
- Never assume that public accessibility means permission to test.
- Never expand an assessment beyond its approved scope.
- Stop testing if authorization is withdrawn.

## 3. Respect for Privacy

- Respect the privacy of system owners, employees and customers.
- Use synthetic or approved test data wherever possible.
- Do not access, copy or disclose private information unnecessarily.
- Redact personal information from screenshots and reports.
- Protect all assessment evidence from unauthorized access.

## 4. Responsible Security Testing

- Use controlled, proportionate testing methods.
- Avoid destructive activities and service disruption.
- Do not perform denial-of-service attacks.
- Do not install malware or establish unauthorized persistence.
- Do not bypass access controls outside the approved test plan.
- Do not exploit discovered weaknesses beyond what is necessary and authorized to validate them.

## 5. AI Security Responsibilities

- Do not use prompt injection to access another person's private information.
- Do not attempt to extract confidential data from real users or production systems.
- Protect model credentials, API keys and system configuration details.
- Test AI guardrails using controlled and harmless examples.
- Do not use AI-generated output as unquestioned evidence.
- Verify important findings independently.
- Require human approval for sensitive or consequential actions.

## 6. Confidentiality

- Treat client information as confidential.
- Never publish client findings without written approval.
- Do not share credentials, tokens or private configuration details.
- Store assessment records securely.
- Follow agreed retention and secure deletion requirements.

## 7. Honesty and Professional Integrity

- Report findings accurately.
- Never fabricate vulnerabilities, evidence or test results.
- Distinguish confirmed findings from theoretical risks.
- Explain assessment limitations.
- Never guarantee that a system is completely secure.
- Never exaggerate qualifications, experience or certifications.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.

## 8. Responsible Disclosure

- Report security weaknesses privately to the authorized contact.
- Provide clear evidence and practical remediation advice.
- Agree on disclosure timelines with the system owner.
- Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities without appropriate authorization.

## 9. Legal and Regulatory Responsibilities

- Follow applicable laws and regulations in the jurisdiction where the assessment takes place.
- Respect contractual obligations and data protection requirements.
- Obtain appropriate approval before handling regulated or sensitive information.
- Seek qualified legal guidance where obligations are unclear.

## 10. Professional Commitment

I commit to:

- Acting ethically and responsibly.
- Respecting authorization and assessment boundaries.
- Protecting client information.
- Maintaining professional competence.
- Communicating honestly.
- Using cybersecurity knowledge to protect systems and people.
